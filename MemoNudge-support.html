How to Use

Three steps to never
miss a moment again

🔔
Step 01

Create a Reminder

Give it a name

Tap "+", enter a title, pick a date and time. Optionally add a note. That's it — your reminder is ready to be scheduled.

Step 02

Choose a Repeat Pattern

One-time, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly

Pick from five flexible repeat types. Set custom weekdays for weekly reminders, or pick the Nth weekday of every month — flexible enough for anything.

Step 03

Get Notified & Snooze

You're in control

When it's time, you'll get a local notification. Can't deal with it right now? Tap Snooze and pick a custom delay — 5, 10, 30 minutes, whatever fits.
